# Experiments log

Every experiment, also the unsuccessful ones. This log feeds report section 12.

The project was first developed for the grade-5 scope (08–12.11.2026: thick + thin lines,
two frequency paths, v(t), clustering). On 19.11.2026 it was reduced to the **grade-4 scope**:
thick lines and one velocity per object (plan.md, "Scope change"). Entries #1–#18 come from the
first phase. They are kept because they justify steps that are still part of the method. Their
metrics were measured then:

- *pixel AUC* of the enhanced image against the synthetic ground truth, "light" = car/van,
  "heavy" = tram/truck, for hf / lf / fused maps;
- or object counts / F1 of the grade-5 detector.

Entries about the removed parts (thin-line path, the low-frequency path, ridge tracking and
v(t), clustering) were deleted from this log. Entries #19–#25 are the grade-4 phase, measured
with `scripts/run_experiments.py` and the grade-4 scoring.
